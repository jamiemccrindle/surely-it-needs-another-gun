Your first unreasonable ship

  1. Power it. Select Reactor Room and draw a closed cyan loop. More area supports more sustained fire. Each ship needs its own supply.
  2. Connect it. Draw armour touching the gold bridge, then attach weapons and engines to that armour. Grey parts are offline. Eraser removes whole pieces; Undo brings them back.
  3. Arm it. Draw red missile racks. Longer strokes launch bigger salvos. Keep their outward exit clear of armour. While frozen, hover a mount for a firing check, or use Test Fire on a safe copy.
  4. Move it. Add engines to each ship. Bigger ships need more thrust. Escorts are separate outlines that you equip just like your flagship.
  5. Make trouble. Send in an attack. Space switches between drawing and automatic flight. You manage the design; the fleet fights itself.
  6. Get paid. Rebuild. Go bigger. Connected green tractors collect Warshards automatically while time runs. When enemies are gone, Fast Restore spends them to rebuild your pre-battle fleet.

Scroll to pan · Pinch to zoom · F to fit the fleet. Shields and point defence are optional ways to survive bigger fights. Friendly fire can hurt other ships when switched on; weapons never damage their own ship.
